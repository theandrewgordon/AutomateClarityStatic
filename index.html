When growth gets messy

More clients can mean more information to track, more handoffs, and more questions only the owner can answer. Files live in different places. Processes live in people's heads. You know things could work better, but it's hard to know where to start.

What we work on

  • Understand your workflows. Map how work gets done and find the bottlenecks, repeat tasks, and unclear handoffs.
  • Document your processes. Turn everyday know-how into simple standard operating procedures (SOPs) and checklists your team can use.
  • Organize your information. Give shared files and institutional knowledge a clear home so people can find what they need.
  • Make your tools work better. Review what you already use, simplify the setup, and identify useful connections before adding more software.
  • Get ready for AI. Choose practical uses, consider what information is appropriate to share, and decide where human review belongs.

How I help

We start by looking at how your business operates today. Together, we choose the most useful improvements and put them into a clear, prioritized plan.

The focus is advice, a limited amount of initial setup, and hands-on training. That could mean organizing a shared folder, documenting a key process, or trying one useful AI workflow together. We agree on the scope before work begins.

Full implementation and ongoing automation management are outside this initial scope. You'll leave with practical next steps and guidance your team can use to keep improving.

Who this is for

Small business owners who want to grow without every task and decision running through them. You may already have useful tools and a capable team, but need help bringing your processes, information, and technology together.
